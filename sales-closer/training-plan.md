# 30-Day Training Plan

For beginners and business owners who want measurable improvement. Adjust to the user's time (about 30-60 minutes per day).

## Setup (Day 0)

- Write the one-sentence promise (`offer-and-value.md`).
- Create a call tracker: date, buyer, showed, qualified, offer given, outcome, objection, next step.
- Record calls (with permission) or take notes right after.

## Week 1: Foundations

| Day | Focus | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mindset | Read `mindset.md`; write your three certainties list |
| 2 | Offer clarity | Write promise, 3 deliverables, proof, guarantee |
| 3 | Discovery | Write 10 questions; practice aloud |
| 4 | Role-play | Friendly buyer, discovery only |
| 5 | Scripts | Write your DM opener and call-booking message |
| 6 | Outreach | Send 10 real messages |
| 7 | Review | Track results; note what worked |

## Week 2: Calls and offers

| Day | Focus | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | Frame and flow | Practice the call frame aloud 5 times |
| 9 | Quantifying | Write 3 ways to put numbers on a buyer's problem |
| 10 | Presenting | Role-play pitch, 3-minute limit |
| 11 | Price delivery | Practice saying your price calmly, then stop |
| 12 | Live calls | Run 2-3 real calls |
| 13 | Self-review | Score each call with the rubric |
| 14 | Weekly review | Choose the top weakness to fix |

## Week 3: Objections and closing

| Day | Focus | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | Objections | Write your answers to the 5 most common objections |
| 16 | Gauntlet | Role-play objection gauntlet, realistic level |
| 17 | Closing | Practice 3 close styles |
| 18 | Follow-up | Write the full follow-up sequence |
| 19 | Live calls | 3 real calls, focus on asking directly |
| 20 | Review | Score and compare to Week 2 |
| 21 | Weekly review | Update scripts based on real objections |

## Week 4: Systems and scale

| Day | Focus | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | Pricing | Revisit price and package structure |
| 23 | Proof | Collect 2-3 testimonials or case notes |
| 24 | Tough buyer | Role-play tough level |
| 25 | Outreach volume | Send 20 messages, follow up on old ones |
| 26 | Live calls | 3-5 real calls |
| 27 | Autopsy | Review lost deals, write lessons |
| 28 | Referrals | Ask past clients for introductions |
| 29 | Refine | Update scripts, offer and tracker |
| 30 | Review | Compare Day 1 to Day 30 metrics; set next goals |

## Metrics to track

- Messages sent
- Reply rate
- Calls booked
- Show rate
- Close rate
- Average deal size
- Common objection
- Rubric score trend

## Benchmarks (rough, vary by industry)

- Show rate: 60-80% with reminders
- Close rate on qualified calls: 20-40% for a trained seller
- Reply rate to cold outreach: 5-15% with good personalization

Use these as references, not guarantees.

## Weekly review questions

1. What worked this week?
2. What did buyers say most often?
3. Where did deals stall?
4. What one change will I test next week?

## If progress stalls

- Too few conversations? Increase outreach.
- Calls but no closes? Review discovery and offer.
- Closes but low price? Revisit value and pricing.
- Closes but refunds? Fix expectations and delivery.
