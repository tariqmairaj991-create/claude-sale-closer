# Scripts and Templates

Always adapt to the user's voice, industry and buyer. These are starting points. Replace brackets with real details.

## 1. Principles for any message

- Short. Specific. About them, not you.
- One clear ask per message.
- Sound like a person, not a brochure.
- Reference something real about the recipient.

## 2. Cold DM / email openers

### Value-first
> Hi [Name], I noticed [specific observation about their business]. I've been helping [similar businesses] with [result]. Mind if I share an idea on how it could apply to you?

### Question opener
> Hi [Name], quick question: how are you currently handling [problem]? I help [type of business] with this and had a thought.

### Referral opener
> Hi [Name], [mutual contact] suggested I reach out. They mentioned you're working on [goal]. Could I share a quick idea?

### Compliment + curiosity
> Hi [Name], loved [specific thing]. Curious, what's your biggest challenge in [area] right now?

## 3. Booking a call

> Sounds like there might be a fit. Want to hop on a 15-minute call this week so I can ask a few questions and see if I can help? Does Tuesday at 2 or Wednesday at 11 work?

Offering two options is easier to answer than "when are you free?"

## 4. Call confirmation (reduces no-shows)

> Hi [Name], confirming our call tomorrow at [time]. To make the most of it, think about [one question]. If anything changes, let me know. Looking forward to it!

Send 24 hours before and 1 hour before.

## 5. Opening a call

> Thanks for making time. Here's what I'd like to do: ask a few questions about what's happening, tell you honestly if I can help and, if so, show you how. Fair?

## 6. Offer presentation (spoken)

> From what you told me, your goal is [goal], and the main thing stopping you is [obstacle], costing around [number]. Here's how we'd handle it: first [step], then [step], then [step]. [Client] had the same issue and got [result] in [time]. And to remove the risk, [guarantee]. The investment is [price]. How does that sound?

## 7. Follow-up messages

### Same day
> Thanks for the conversation today. Quick recap: you want [goal], and we agreed [next step]. Let me know if you have any questions.

### Day 2-3 (add value)
> Hi [Name], thinking about what you said about [concern]. Here's a short case/example that might help: [link or summary]. Happy to answer anything.

### Day 7
> Hi [Name], wanted to check in on the decision. What questions are still open?

### Final
> Hi [Name], I don't want to keep bothering you. Is this still something you want to solve? If now isn't the time, no problem. I'll check back in [month].

## 8. Re-engaging a cold lead

> Hi [Name], it's been a while. Last time we talked, you were working on [goal]. How's that going? I have a new idea that might help.

## 9. Asking for a referral

> I really enjoyed working with you. Who else do you know facing [same problem] that I might help? An intro would mean a lot.

## 10. Asking for a testimonial

> Would you be willing to share a few lines about your experience? Specifically, what was the situation before, what changed, and what result you got.

## 11. Handling a price question in DMs

> Happy to share. It depends on [factors], so a short call lets me give you an accurate number and make sure it's the right fit. Do you have 10 minutes tomorrow?

## 12. Declining a poor-fit prospect

> Thanks for sharing so much. I don't think I'm the right fit for [reason], and I'd rather say so than waste your time. I'd suggest [alternative]. Happy to stay in touch.

## 13. One-liner pitch formula

"I help [who] get [result] in [time] without [pain]."

## 14. Elevator answer to "What do you do?"

> "I help [who] with [problem], so they can [result]. For example, [short case]."
