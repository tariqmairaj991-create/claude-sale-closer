# Call Review

Use when the user shares a transcript, recording notes or a description of a call.

## 1. Gather context

If missing, ask briefly: what's the offer and price, who was the buyer, what was the outcome, and which part felt off?

## 2. Scoring rubric (1-5 each)

| Area | 1 (weak) | 3 (okay) | 5 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opening and frame** | Jumped in, no structure | Brief intro | Clear agenda, relaxed tone, buyer agreed |
| **Discovery** | Few or closed questions, pitched early | Some depth | Rich questions, quantified problem, found why now |
| **Qualification** | No budget, decision or timeline check | Partial | Full picture, honest fit decision |
| **Reflect and bridge** | None | Loose summary | Accurate summary, buyer said "exactly" |
| **Offer presentation** | Feature dump | Reasonable | Tied to their words, proof, clear path |
| **Price delivery** | Apologetic, rushed, discounted early | Clear but wordy | Calm, confident, followed by silence |
| **Objection handling** | Argued or caved | Responded without clarifying | Clarified, resolved real concern, checked |
| **Close** | No ask | Soft ask | Direct ask, clear decision |
| **Next step** | Vague | Verbal | Booked, dated, confirmed |
| **Tone and listening** | Talked over, monologues | Mostly attentive | Curious, balanced, warm |

Total out of 50. Interpret: under 25 needs fundamentals, 25-37 solid base with gaps, 38+ strong.

## 3. Metrics to calculate if possible

- **Talk ratio:** seller vs buyer speaking time (target: buyer 60-70% in discovery).
- **Questions asked:** count and type (open vs closed).
- **Time to price:** when price was mentioned.
- **Objections raised** and how each was handled.
- **Whether a next step was set.**

## 4. Review output format

1. **Verdict (1-2 sentences):** overall assessment and likely reason for the outcome.
2. **What went well:** 1-2 moments, quoted.
3. **Biggest leaks:** 3 specific issues, each with:
   - What happened (quote)
   - Why it hurt
   - Rewritten version of the line
4. **Missed opportunities:** buying signals ignored, deeper questions not asked.
5. **Score table.**
6. **Practice drill** for the next week.

## 5. Common patterns and fixes

| Pattern | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer says "send me info" | Weak discovery, no urgency | Quantify the problem and book next step before ending |
| Price shock | Value not built | Restate the cost of the problem before price |
| Long silence after pitch | Overexplaining | Shorter pitch, direct ask |
| Buyer ghosts after call | No next step booked | Always schedule before hanging up |
| Seller talks 70%+ | Pitching instead of asking | Prepare 8 questions; use echo and pause |
| Many "I'll think about it" | Hidden objections | Clarify early: "What would stop you?" |
| Discounting too fast | Fear of losing deal | Trade scope, not price |

## 6. Lost-deal autopsy

For a lost deal, answer:
1. Was the buyer qualified?
2. Did they feel understood?
3. Was the problem costed?
4. Was the offer matched to their goal?
5. What was the real objection?
6. What would I do differently?

Write the lesson in one sentence and apply it to the next call.

## 7. Tone of feedback

Be honest and constructive. Name specifics, not vague praise or criticism. End with one thing to practice next.
