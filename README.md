# Claude Sales Closer

A deep Claude skill that coaches business owners and sales beginners through the whole sales process: finding and shaping an offer, discovery, pricing, objections, closing, follow-up, role-play practice and call review.

## What it does

- **Learn:** teaches selling principles in plain language
- **Prepare:** builds a plan for your next call
- **Offer and pricing:** diagnoses weak offers, structures pricing and guarantees
- **Scripts:** DMs, cold outreach, follow-ups, referral and testimonial asks
- **Objections:** a playbook for 15+ common objections
- **Role-play:** realistic buyer personas at three difficulty levels
- **Review:** scores your call transcripts and rewrites weak moments
- **Plan:** a 30-day practice plan with metrics

## Install

1. Download or clone this repo.
2. Zip the `sales-closer` folder (the zip must contain the folder with `SKILL.md` at its root).
3. In Claude, open Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills, and upload the zip.
4. Try: "Prep me for a call with a restaurant owner" or "Let me practice handling price objections."

## Structure

```
sales-closer/
├── SKILL.md               # Router: intake, modes, standards
├── mindset.md             # Certainty, ethics, language
├── offer-and-value.md     # Building and fixing offers
├── pricing-and-guarantees.md
├── discovery.md           # Question sequence and qualification
├── closing.md             # Call flow, closes, follow-up
├── objections.md          # Playbook and prevention
├── scripts.md             # DMs, emails, follow-ups
├── call-types.md          # High-ticket, local, B2B, inbound, outbound
├── roleplay.md            # Personas, drills, feedback
├── call-review.md         # Rubric and review format
└── training-plan.md       # 30-day plan and metrics
```
